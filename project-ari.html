Environmental technology

Project ARI.

IoTSustainabilityConcept stage

Technical & entrepreneurial case study

Technical architecture

Combines an Arduino and ESP32-CAM enabled collection vessel with waste capture, filtration and a monitoring interface for observing waterway conditions.

Operating model

Frames clean-up as a repeatable service: identify a waterway, deploy the device, monitor collection and communicate outcomes to local stakeholders.

Business value

Creates a potential environmental-technology offering for communities, sponsors and facilities managers seeking visible, data-supported waterway care.

Next validation

Test buoyancy, propulsion, collection capacity, battery performance, water exposure and the usefulness of monitoring data in a real operating environment.
