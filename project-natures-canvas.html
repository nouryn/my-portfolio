Technical & entrepreneurial case study

Technical architecture

Explores an Arduino-controlled paper-processing workflow that adapts a shredder and integrates the steps required to transform waste paper into seed-paper sheets.

Operating model

Converts discarded paper into a usable, plantable product through a simple collection, processing, drying and distribution cycle.

Business value

Could serve schools, events and sustainability-minded organisations looking for low-waste stationery, gifting or educational products.

Next validation

Test paper-to-seed ratios, sheet durability, seed viability, processing consistency, unit cost and repeatability at a larger production volume.
