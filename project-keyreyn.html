Technical & Entrepreneurial Case Study

Technical architecture

Designed a contactless payment flow centred on iris recognition, with ESP32-CAM proposed as the image-capture layer and a verification workflow before payment authorisation.

Product problem

Targets the trade-off between stronger identity verification and a low-friction checkout experience in digital payments.

Business value

Positions biometric authentication as a trust feature for payment providers and merchants seeking to reduce account misuse while protecting user convenience.

Next validation

Prototype image capture, assess matching accuracy and liveness controls, map privacy requirements, and test the customer journey with representative users.
